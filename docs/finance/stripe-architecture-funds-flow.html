Flows
1
Simplr → Tenant: invoice email on the 1st (pay link, full balance) · reminder on the 4th if unpaid.
2
Tenant → Simplr → Stripe: tenant opens the link; Simplr creates a Checkout Session server-side on Property Pro's Stripe account.
3
Tenant → Stripe-hosted Checkout: rent paid by credit card. Debit/prepaid declined. Disclosed credit-card surcharge (pending counsel review). Card data never touches Simplr.
4
Stripe → Simplr: signature-verified webhooks (checkout.session.completed, refunds, disputes) → ledger updated, receipt issued. Success page shows “processing” only.
5
Stripe → Property Pro bank account: payouts.
6
Property Pro bank account → Property owners: monthly owner statement: rent collected less management fee & expenses (existing process).
7
Tenant → Property Pro office: cash or check in person (always available).
8
Property Pro office / accountant → Simplr: accountant records the payment → same ledger, receipt issued.
9
Property Pro office → Property Pro bank account: deposits.
10
Planned: ACH bank payments with faster settlement (~2 business days): being explored, not in launch scope.
Key facts
  • Simplr never holds or moves funds. There is no Stripe Connect or platform account.
  • Property Pro owns the Stripe account. Simplr gets sandbox-only developer access until production review.
  • No autopay and no saved payment methods at launch. Tenants make a new payment each month.
  • Rent only at launch. Security deposits are handled outside Stripe.
  • Fees: surcharge on credit cards only, subject to counsel review. No surcharge on debit or prepaid cards.
